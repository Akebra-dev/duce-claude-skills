# Duce Atelier brand

Apply this whenever the work should read as Duce Atelier.

## Palette (exact tokens)
- Ink `#0A0A0A` — dominant background. Never pure black.
- Signal Red `#FF0013` — the one accent: CTAs and the diagonal motif. Use sparingly.
- Paper `#F2F0EC` — light section background. Never pure white.
- White `#FFFFFF` — text on dark grounds.
- Slate `#8C8C8C` — muted text, borders, quiet detail.
- Gold `#C9A24B` — reserved for the "Your VA" product line ONLY. Never elsewhere.

Ink and Paper are the grounds. Signal Red is the only shout.

## Type
- One typeface: Metropolis, worked across weights. Headings black/extrabold with tight tracking; body regular with relaxed line height.
- A monospace utility voice for labels: uppercase, wide letter-spacing (the "cut-sheet" look).

## Signature devices (one angle, minus 7 degrees, reused everywhere)
- The red cut: a single diagonal bar skewed at -7deg down the edge of dark sections.
- Clip corner: a notch cut from the top-right of cards and frames. Angular, never rounded.
- Diagonal tick: a short skewed red bar under headings and beside list items.

## Voice, all rules are hard
- Write like a sharp founder talking to one person: warm, direct, confident, lightly selling, never hype.
- Simple English. Short sentences. Active voice. Sell the outcome, not the feature.
- NEVER use an em dash or en dash. Use a comma or a full stop. This is absolute.
- Say the thing plainly. No buzzwords, no jargon, no filler.

## Do / do not
- Do keep Ink and Paper as the grounds and let Signal Red be the only accent.
- Do reuse the -7deg angle; do give real work room (grayscale photos that warm to colour on hover).
- Do not add new accent colours, round the corners, use emoji as icons, or add drop shadows for drama.
- Do not pass AI images off as real work or real people.
