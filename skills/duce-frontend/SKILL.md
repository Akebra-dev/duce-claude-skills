# Premium front-end design

Turn a plain build into a site that feels intentional and expensive. Apply these
five techniques together on every project, not one at a time. If the project has
a brand (for a Duce project, see the `duce-brand` skill), the brand's colours and
fonts win, and you use these techniques to execute that brand well. For the full
setup, stack, and build order, pair this with the `duce-10k-website` skill.

## 1. Control the mood, advanced dark mode

- Define a clear base, never plain black: deep charcoal, off-black, or
  "Bloomberg terminal black".
- Pick **one** dominant accent (emerald teal, electric blue, amber).
- Use the accent **only** on CTAs, active nav states, and hover. Never on
  headings, body copy, or random decoration.
- Set the mood with a real-world reference, for example "a high-end watchmaker's
  workshop at night" or "a luxury whiskey bar at closing time".

## 2. Typography is the design system

- Avoid the giveaway defaults used on their own: Inter, Roboto, Open Sans. Pair a
  characterful display face with a clean body face:
  - Luxury or editorial: Playfair Display headings with Inter body.
  - SaaS or corporate: Poppins with Work Sans.
  - Startup or landing: DM Serif Display with Inter.
  - Designed or modern: Satoshi, or Cabinet Grotesk.
  - Neo-brutalist: Clash Display (bold, wide tracking) with IBM Plex Mono for UI.
- Build a strong hierarchy: headings and body clearly differ in size, weight, and
  contrast. Typography is the design system.

## 3. Whitespace is the primary directive

- Macro: increase section gaps, card padding, and the breathing room between
  major blocks.
- Micro: set line height, letter spacing, and paragraph gaps on purpose.
- Rule of thumb: if in doubt, add more space. Calm space reads as expensive, the
  way Apple and Linear feel.

## 4. The five-point hero

A hero is judged in well under a second, so every hero carries all five:

1. **Outcome headline**: the customer's result, not a description of the business.
2. **Specific sub-headline**: one clear layer of specificity about the result.
3. **Purposeful CTA**: action copy like "Claim your first week free", never
   "Get started".
4. **Product visual**: a real screenshot or product shot, not abstract stock art.
5. **Trust signal**: a concrete marker, a logo bar, a customer count, or a real
   testimonial.

## 5. The 60-30-10 colour system

- **60% dominant**: background and main surfaces.
- **30% secondary**: cards, navigation, dividers, supporting surfaces.
- **10% accent**: links, highlights, and CTAs only.
- Contrast: at least **4.5:1 for normal text** and **3:1 for large text**.

## The kickoff directive, fill the brackets

```
Act as a premium web designer. Do not use default AI layouts. Apply five techniques:
1. Use [visual reference] to set the mood, with a 60-30-10 colour system.
2. Use [specific font pairing] with high hierarchy contrast.
3. Treat whitespace as the primary directive, maximise macro and micro gaps.
4. Build a hero with an outcome headline, a specific sub-headline, a purposeful CTA, a real product visual, and a trust signal.
5. Meet 4.5:1 contrast on all text.
```

## Copy voice

Sell the outcome, not the feature. Never use an em dash or an en dash, use a comma
or a full stop.
